About

Special Collections is a digital space designed and developed by the Université de Montréal Libraries. It provides access to a selection of rare books, manuscripts, images, and other materials held in their collections.

By making it possible to examine these documents closely and to explore them beyond the University’s walls, it opens up new possibilities for discovery, research, and teaching. The result of a major technological and documentary undertaking, it is built on free and open-source software and open standards, including IIIF. It offers features that allow users to appreciate the full richness of the works and their content.

Thanks to the support of the Université de Montréal, the Libraries are carrying out a major digitization initiative to increase the proportion of their special collections available online. This digital space will continue to grow as the work progresses. It currently presents a portion of the collections; for a more comprehensive search, please consult the Sofia search tool.

How to use the platform

Our Collections

The collections of the Université de Montréal libraries are developed in support of teaching and research at the university.

In addition to building general collections reflecting the evolution of knowledge and ideas, the libraries also fulfill a documentary preservation mandate. Their special collections include nearly 150,000 documents that are rare, old, or precious, bearing witness to the evolution of knowledge, disciplines, and research.

Learn more about the UdeM libraries collections

Why digitize the collections?

Digitization offers another way to discover the collections. It represents a lasting investment by the Université de Montréal Libraries in providing access to documentary heritage and showcasing it. This work makes it possible to explore documents remotely, examine certain details, and consult them without handling them. Making them available online supports research and teaching and opens up new possibilities for comparison and interpretation.

This digital access complements consultation of the originals. Documents held in the special collections have a value that goes beyond their content. A binding, an annotation, or a provenance mark can reveal how a work was made, used, and passed down. On site, the format, weight, paper, and texture add another dimension to discovery: that of the book as an object, bearing its own history.

Using Documents on This Platform

The resources made available on this platform have been digitized following a rigorous review of the copyright associated with each item.

By making resources available on this platform, the libraries grant users a licence permitting consultation, downloading, and use of these resources for study, academic research, or private non-commercial purposes.

Any commercial use, mass distribution, or sharing requires a separate licence, which you may obtain by contacting the Direction des collections, du traitement documentaire et des métadonnées.

Citing a Document from This Platform

When using a document from this platform, you must cite its source. For more details on citation formats, consult the Cite Your Sources page in the library toolkit.
